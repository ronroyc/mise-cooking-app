Mise helps you keep track of recipes and the ingredients you have at home. It is not a commercial service, and there is no paid plan.

AI suggestions

A later version of Mise will offer ingredient substitution suggestions generated by an AI model. These suggestions can be wrong. Treat them as ideas, not instructions, and never rely on them for allergy or food safety decisions.
