Some sites block this

Big sites like Allrecipes and Serious Eats don't let apps read their pages. For those, drag this button to Safari's Favorites bar: Save to Slice'd

Then open a recipe in Safari and click it. Slice'd opens with the recipe filled in, ready to check and save.

If clicking it does nothing: in Safari, open Settings, Advanced, and turn on "Show features for web developers". Then in the Develop menu, turn on "Allow JavaScript from Smart Search Field".
